Privacy policy.

Last updated: November 8, 2026

What we collect

  • Your email address, and if you sign in with Google, your Google account's basic profile (name, email, profile picture).
  • The planner data you enter: events, routines, to-dos, groups and completed checks.
  • If you turn on reminders: your device's push address and time zone, used only to send those reminders. Signing out removes it.

How we use it

Only to sign you in and to save and sync your planner across your devices. We do not sell, share or use your data for advertising.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in) and the site is hosted on Vercel. Each account can only read and change its own planner.

Deleting your data

Email ttttang0923@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, and your account and planner will be deleted.
